Ecosystem

Aramoon students don't just study tooling - cohort projects graduate into real internal platforms used across the institute.

Alumni network

Graduates stay connected through mentorship pairings, referral programs, and an annual cohort demo day with hiring partners.

Hiring partners

Regional employers across networking, software engineering and security recruit directly from graduating cohorts.

Internal tooling

Web-tooling cohort projects (dashboards, previewers, internal utilities) are maintained by the current cohort and handed off to the next one every semester.

Community CTF

A running internal capture-the-flag lab modeling realistic misconfigurations, used by the cybersecurity track.
